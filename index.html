    The STOPP Skill

    A CBT-based grounding technique to pause, gain perspective, and choose how to proceed when feeling overwhelmed.

    S - Stop! Pause immediately. Do not react on impulse.
    T - Take a breath Slow down your breathing. Inhale deep into your belly.
    O - Observe What thoughts am I having? What sensations are in my body?
    P - Pull Back Put it in perspective. Will this matter in a week? What's the bigger picture?
    P - Practice / Proceed What is the best thing to do right now for my wellbeing?
