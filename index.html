The S.T.O.P. Technique

A quick grounding exercise when feeling overwhelmed.

S — Stop

Pause what you are doing.

T — Take a Breath

Focus on a slow, deep breath.

O — Observe

Notice thoughts and feelings without judgment.

P — Proceed

Ask: "What is most helpful right now?"
