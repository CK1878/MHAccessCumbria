    The STOPP Grounding Skill

    A simple CBT-based grounding technique to pause, gain perspective, and avoid impulsive actions.

    S - Stop! Pause immediately. Notice the urge to react.
    T - Take a breath Slow down your breathing. Inhale deeply into your belly.
    O - Observe What thoughts am I focusing on? What physical sensations do I feel?
    P - Pull Back Put it in perspective. What is the bigger picture here?
    P - Proceed / Practice What is the most helpful thing to do right now for my wellbeing?
