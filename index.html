The S.T.O.P. Technique

A quick grounding exercise when feeling overwhelmed or triggered.

S — Stop

Pause whatever you are doing. Put things on hold for a moment.

T — Take a Breath

Focus on a slow deep breath. Breathe in calm, breathe out tension.

O — Observe

Notice your thoughts, physical feelings, and surroundings non-judgmentally.

P — Proceed

Ask yourself: "What is the most helpful thing I can do right now?"
